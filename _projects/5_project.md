**Keywords:** electrolyte jet machining (EJM) · electrochemical jet processing · programmable surface texturing · microstructure / crystallographic texture · **Texture Jet Ltd.**

Electrolyte Jet Processing (EJP) is a non-contact, electrochemical jet-based approach to **machining and functional surface texturing**. In this area, our work has focused on turning EJM from a “single-feature” laboratory process into a **repeatable, programmable process family**—with control over feature placement, geometry, and surface integrity.

Core themes:
- **Process physics + control:** linking jet delivery, address angle, and energy distribution to outcomes.
- **Programmable surface structuring:** translating toolpath concepts into reliable micro-scale surface features.
- **Materials sensitivity:** understanding how alloy microstructure influences removal behaviour and surface quality.
- **Industrialisation:** technology translation and application-led development through **Texture Jet Ltd.**
