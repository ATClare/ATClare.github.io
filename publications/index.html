Research Output

Publications

Publications and selected papers across manufacturing engineering, process innovation, and advanced processing.

Publications are listed in reverse chronological order and reflect the lab’s work across additive manufacturing, electrochemical jet processing, structured materials, surface engineering, and process capability.

The publication record spans both fundamental and applied work, with a strong emphasis on process understanding, measurement, materials response, and engineering routes to more reliable manufacturing outcomes.
