    </p>

    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="content-card">
    <h2>Teaching Style</h2>
    <p>
      Classes are strongest when they connect principle to practice. That means using examples, project work, and process-led
      thinking to show how engineering knowledge is used in real decisions rather than isolated from them.
    </p>
    <ul class="feature-list">
      <li>problem-based learning</li>
      <li>application-led explanation</li>
      <li>design and interpretation</li>
      <li>research-informed examples</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
