<div class="content-band">
  <h2>What Work in the Group Looks Like</h2>
  <p>
    Projects typically combine theory, experiments, hardware, and analysis. That can mean designing rigs, running process
    studies, measuring outcomes, interpreting results, and turning those findings into better manufacturing methods.
  </p>
</div>
