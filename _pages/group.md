## Join the group

We’re always happy to hear from motivated students and researchers interested in manufacturing and process innovation.

**Opportunities include:**
- **Extended research visits** (undergraduate, MSc, PhD, postdoctoral)
- **Graduate study** (MSc / PhD)
- **Co-supervised projects** with partner institutions

If you’re thinking of applying, send a short email with:
- a **CV**
- a paragraph on **your interests** and which topics you’d like to work on
- any relevant **portfolio / GitHub / papers**

Email: **adam.clare@ubc.ca**
